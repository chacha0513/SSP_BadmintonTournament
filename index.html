ℹ️ How Matches Are Generated ▶

Skill tiers, not manual ratings. Each player's ⚡ number comes purely from where they're listed in Team Setup — Player 1 is the strongest, the last player is the weakest. Whatever your team size, that roster splits into 5 skill tiers from strongest to weakest, with players near the top of the list landing in the highest tier and players near the bottom in the lowest. No one has to guess a rating — just list your team from best to worst.

Every game is exactly balanced. The two doubles pairs in any game are guaranteed to have the same combined tier total (e.g. 9 vs 9, 7 vs 7) — every single time, by construction. Since both teams share the same tier makeup, whatever total one team's pair adds up to, the opponent is mathematically guaranteed to have players available to match it exactly.

Composition still varies. Which two teammates get paired together, and which specific opponents they face, is randomized fresh every time you generate — so it's not the same predictable "strongest with weakest" pattern every week, even though the balance stays perfect.

Everyone plays fairly. Each player gets exactly 4 games (2 against each opposing team), never repeats a partner within the same matchup, and the scheduler actively spreads each team's games evenly across early, middle, and late rounds — so no team is stuck playing all its matches in the first few rounds while another coasts in late.
